🏟️ Meet Al-Noor Sports Club

All screenshots and walkthroughs in this guide follow Al-Noor Sports Club — a fictional football and swimming club managing 45 students across 3 teams. The admin team uses Club Management to register students, schedule weekly training sessions, track daily attendance, and collect monthly subscription fees. Parents log in to follow their children's progress and pay invoices with one tap.

The sample data in the demo account reflects a typical week at Al-Noor — real-looking names, events, and payments — so every feature works out of the box when you explore it.
